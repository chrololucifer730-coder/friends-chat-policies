1. Overview

Friends Chat Module is a communication application operated by Aman under the project/company name COCHAT. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may collect, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Account information: email address, password credentials handled through the authentication system, account identifiers, verification and recovery information.
  • Profile information: display name, username, profile photo, biography, status and privacy settings.
  • Communication content: messages, reactions, replies, group content, status content and media that users choose to send or upload.
  • Contacts: contact information only when the user grants the relevant permission and uses contact-discovery features. The app should use privacy-preserving matching and avoid unnecessary full-address-book storage.
  • Media and device permissions: files, photos/videos, camera, microphone and location only when the user chooses a feature that requires them and grants the relevant permission.
  • Technical information: device model, Android/app version, diagnostics, crash information, timestamps, network/connection information and security-related logs.
  • Support information: bug reports, feedback, screenshots, attachments and contact information voluntarily supplied to support.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, deliver and synchronize messages, provide groups/status/call features, send notifications, process media, provide support, prevent abuse, enforce permissions, investigate reports, improve reliability and comply with legal obligations.

4. Private Communications

Private messages and other user content are used to provide the communication features requested by the user. Access to private content by service personnel, if technically necessary for support or moderation, must be limited by role and authorization. Passwords, authentication tokens and other secrets must never be stored in plaintext or exposed to moderators.

5. Google Sheets and Operational Records

If Google Sheets is used for operational support records, it should contain only limited administrative metadata such as user ID, email, account creation date, recovery request ID/status, support notes, timestamps, assigned moderator and resolution status. Passwords, authentication tokens, private message contents and other secrets must not be stored there.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers required to operate the app, such as authentication, database, file storage, push notification, email, analytics or calling providers. The actual providers used by the production app should be listed or linked here before publication if required by applicable law or platform policy.

7. Security

The service is designed to use encrypted network connections, secure authentication/session handling, server-side authorization, access-controlled media, rate limiting, input validation, role-based permissions and audit logging. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed to operate the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, maintain security, or complete legitimate support and moderation processes. Users may request account deletion through the app or the designated external deletion process.

9. Account Deletion

Users should be able to start deletion from Settings → Account → Delete Account. An external deletion method should also be provided for users who cannot access the app. Deletion may require reasonable identity/account verification. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or other legitimate purposes.

10. User Controls

Depending on the features implemented, users can manage profile visibility, contact discovery, status privacy, notifications, media permissions, location permissions, sessions, security settings and account deletion.

11. Children

The service is not intended to bypass applicable age requirements. Where applicable law requires parental consent, age restrictions or additional safeguards, those requirements will apply.

12. International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure providers used, information may be processed in countries other than the user's country. Appropriate contractual, technical and organizational safeguards should be used where required by applicable law.

13. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
