What happens

Account deletion is intended to remove or anonymize the account and associated personal information according to the service's retention rules. Some records may be retained for a limited period where required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate audit purposes. Content already shared with other users may remain visible to those users in accordance with the app's implemented deletion rules.
